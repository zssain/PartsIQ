# Recruiter walkthrough script

## Three minute version

First 30 seconds: explain the bilingual enquiry problem and human approval. Show the synthetic-data notice and single-backend architecture. Next 60 seconds: run the mixed-language photo scenario, confirm identifiers, and show compatibility evidence and the draft. Next 30 seconds: approve and display the published reply. Next 30 seconds: demonstrate a missing serial or incompatible part and show the system refusing to guess. Final 30 seconds: show the warehouse split, Snowflake source version and test/audit evidence. State which providers are live and which are fixtures.

## Eight minute version

Explain the problem and roles. Walk through chat, photo and confirmation. Show the fixed workflow and why agent routing is unnecessary. Demonstrate serial ambiguity and an incompatible exact part number. Compare candidate evidence and stock freshness. Edit an item and demonstrate approval invalidation. Approve and export the result. Show Snowflake source/reporting and the validated serving cache. Finish with measured tests, cost controls and single-host limitations.

## Recording checklist

Use synthetic data and clean browser profiles. Hide keys, JWTs, signed URLs and billing identifiers. Verify all four scenarios before recording. Include both languages and one failure path. Do not claim a video exists until recorded. Add captions and check published links. Accuracy claims require denominators; latency claims require actual hardware/runtime context.
